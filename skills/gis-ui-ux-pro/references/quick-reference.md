## Accessibility

- **A01 Text alternative** (critical): Do: Provide a summary and a feature table or list as a text alternative for the map (WCAG 1.1.1). Don't: Do not leave the map as an unlabeled canvas. [source](https://sitepen.com/blog/navigating-accessibility-in-two-dimensions)
- **A02 Keyboard-operable map and features** (critical): Do: Support arrow keys to pan, +/- to zoom and Enter to open features. Don't: Do not make mouse use mandatory. [source](https://leafletjs.com/examples/accessibility/)
- **A03 No keyboard trap; skip link** (critical): Do: Never trap keyboard focus in the map and add a skip link before it. Don't: Do not swallow Tab or Escape inside the map. [source](https://gisyaliny.github.io/webgis/docs/General/WCAG_2_1_AA_Developer_Checklist.html)
- **A04 Visible focus and logical order** (high): Do: Keep visible focus and a logical order: skip link, search, controls, map, panels. Don't: Do not remove the focus outline. [source](https://jimmieego.github.io/2019-uc-a11y-slides)
- **A05 Non-drag alternatives** (high): Do: Provide a non-drag alternative for pan, swipe and the time slider (WCAG 2.5.7). Don't: Do not make dragging the only interaction. [source](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html)
- **A06 Contrast** (high): Do: Text at least 4.5:1; symbols and outlines at least 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). Don't: Do not rely on pale outlines on a busy basemap. [source](https://nsgic.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Making-GIS-Data-Accessible-and-ADA-Compliant.pdf)
- **A07 Never use color alone** (critical): Do: Add an icon, pattern or label in addition to color (WCAG 1.4.1). Don't: Do not encode status or category in color only. [source](https://boia.org/blog/interactive-maps-and-accessibility-4-tips)
- **A08 Accessible names on markers** (high): Do: Give interactive markers an accessible name; make decorative maps inert. Don't: Do not leave focusable unnamed markers. [source](https://github.com/nfreear/leaflet.a11y)
- **A09 prefers-reduced-motion** (medium): Do: Honor prefers-reduced-motion by disabling flyTo, autoplay and pulsing. Don't: Do not autoplay or pulse regardless of the setting. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/api/properties/)
- **A10 Focus management for popups** (high, synth): Do: Move focus into the popup, announce it and return focus on close. Don't: Do not open popups without moving focus.
- **A11 Touch targets** (medium): Do: Make touch targets at least 44 px on mobile (WCAG 2.5.8 sets 24 by 24 CSS px as the minimum at AA). Don't: Do not use tiny map controls on touch devices. [source](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)

## Controls

- **C01 Attribution always visible** (critical): Do: Make attribution visible without interaction. It may collapse after being shown (dismiss, map interaction or after about 5 s) but must stay findable, for example via an (i) button. Any corner is fine; lower right is traditional. Don't: Do not hide attribution behind an 'i' on load. [source](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines)
- **C02 Mapbox logo and text attribution** (critical): Do: Keep both the Mapbox logo and the attribution text on the map (typically logo bottom-left; GL JS places the text bottom-right by default). The logo may be moved but not restyled. Don't: Do not hide, crop or restyle the logo or text, for example with CSS display:none. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/dive-deeper/attribution/)
- **C03 Attribution in Leaflet and OpenLayers** (critical): Do: Add attribution manually when using Mapbox tiles in Leaflet or OpenLayers. Don't: Do not assume these libraries add Mapbox attribution automatically. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/dive-deeper/attribution/)
- **C04 Control corner conventions** (medium, synth): Do: Stack zoom, compass, geolocate and fullscreen top-right, scale bar bottom-left, attribution at the bottom, search top-left or in the header. Don't: Do not scatter controls at random corners or let them overlap attribution. [source](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/location/latest/developerguide/how-to-add-control-on-map.html)
- **C05 Compass only if the map can rotate** (low): Do: Show the compass only if the map can rotate; clicking it resets to north. Don't: Do not show a compass on a north-locked map. [source](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/location/latest/developerguide/how-to-add-control-on-map.html)
- **C06 Scale bar with locale units** (medium): Do: Use metric or imperial to match the locale; offer a toggle where users mix both. Don't: Never show a fixed ratio such as 1:50,000 on a web map. [source](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/location/latest/developerguide/how-to-add-control-on-map.html)
- **C07 Geolocate states and permission** (medium): Do: Show off, locating, tracking and error states. Ask for permission on tap, not on page load. Don't: Do not trigger the browser permission prompt on page load. [source](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/location/latest/developerguide/how-to-add-control-on-map.html)
- **C08 Keyboard shortcuts help** (medium): Do: Provide a keyboard shortcuts help control placed before the map in focus order. Don't: Do not rely on users guessing the map keyboard shortcuts. [source](https://github.com/nfreear/leaflet.a11y)
- **C09 Search or coordinate input as drag alternative** (high): Do: Provide search or coordinate input so the view can be set without dragging. Don't: Do not make dragging the only way to reach a location. [source](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html)
- **C10 Measure and draw tools** (medium): Do: Show area and length live while drawing; keep geometry editable and exportable. Don't: Do not make users draw once and lose the geometry or the value. [source](https://developers.planet.com/tag/explorer3.html)
- **C11 Basemap switcher** (medium, synth): Do: Offer 2 to 4 basemap thumbnails and re-check overlay contrast on each basemap. Don't: Do not offer many basemaps or leave overlays unreadable on some of them. [source](https://carto.com/carto-colors/)

## Symbology

- **S01 Normalize choropleths** (critical): Do: Map rates or densities (per capita, per km2). Don't: Never map raw counts in a choropleth. [source](https://axismaps.com/guide/choropleth)
- **S02 Totals as proportional symbols or dot density** (high): Do: Show totals and counts with proportional symbols or dot density. Don't: Do not show totals through area fills. [source](https://axismaps.com/guide/proportional-symbols)
- **S03 Dot density needs an equal-area projection** (high): Do: Use an equal-area projection for dot density maps. Don't: Do not place dots on Web Mercator across large latitude ranges. [source](https://axismaps.com/guide/dot-density)
- **S04 Number of classes** (high): Do: Use 3 to 7 classes for sequential ramps (CARTO ramps stop at 7; ColorBrewer sequential goes up to 9), at most 11 for diverging, and about 8 or fewer for qualitative (some schemes such as Paired and Set3 go to 12; Set2 only to 8). Don't: Do not use more classes than readers can tell apart. [source](https://cartoframes.readthedocs.io/en/v0.9.2/styling.html)
- **S05 Classification method** (high): Do: Jenks for clustered data on a single map; quantile for rankings or evenly spread data; equal interval for familiar ranges; fixed breaks when maps must be compared. Don't: Do not use per-map automatic breaks when maps are compared side by side. [source](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/help/mapping/layer-properties/data-classification-methods.html)
- **S06 Ramp type matches the data** (high): Do: Sequential for ordered values; diverging only with a meaningful midpoint; qualitative for categories. Don't: Do not use a diverging ramp without a real midpoint or a sequential one for categories. [source](https://carto.com/carto-colors/)
- **S07 Lightness direction follows the basemap** (high): Do: On a light basemap dark means high; on a dark basemap bright means high. Don't: Do not reuse the same ramp direction on both basemaps. [source](https://carto.com/carto-colors/)
- **S08 No rainbow or jet ramps** (high): Do: Use perceptually ordered ramps (viridis-like, single-hue sequential) for continuous data. Don't: Do not use rainbow or jet color ramps for continuous data. [source](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/blogs/elegantfigures/2013/08/12/subtleties-of-color-part-3-of-6/)
- **S09 Colorblind-safe palettes** (critical): Do: Use ColorBrewer colorblind-safe schemes (YlGnBu, YlOrRd, Blues, Greens, RdBu, BrBG, PuOr) or CARTO Safe. Check Set2 only for 3 classes. Don't: Never encode meaning with red and green alone. [source](https://carto.com/carto-colors/)
- **S10 Muted basemap, strong data** (high): Do: Mute the basemap (low saturation, light or dark gray) so the data layer is the strongest element. Don't: Do not put saturated thematic colors on a busy basemap. [source](https://go.mapbox.com/rs/117-NXK-490/images/the-guide-to-map-design.pdf)
- **S11 Reveal detail progressively by zoom** (medium): Do: Show coarse features at low zoom and add detail and labels as users zoom in. Don't: Do not show all detail at every zoom. [source](https://go.mapbox.com/rs/117-NXK-490/images/the-guide-to-map-design.pdf)
- **S12 Use color only for data differences** (medium): Do: Use color only to encode data differences, and use the same color for the same meaning everywhere. Don't: Do not use color decoratively or give one color two meanings. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **S13 Minimal legend** (medium): Do: Single column for a ramp, units labeled, no entries for hidden layers; drop the legend if direct labels are enough. Don't: Do not list every layer or show legend entries for hidden layers. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/product/mapping/tips-for-legend-design)
- **S14 Legend reflects what is drawn** (medium, synth): Do: Show only visible, in-scale layers and reflect the current filter. Don't: Do not keep a static legend that disagrees with the map. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/product/mapping/tips-for-legend-design)
- **S15 Heatmap vs choropleth** (medium): Do: Use heatmaps for event intensity and choropleths for rates per unit. Don't: Never ask users to read exact values off a heatmap. [source](https://carto.com/blog/popular-thematic-map-types-techniques-spatial-data)
- **S16 Cluster dense points at low zoom** (high): Do: Cluster dense points at low zoom; show a count and set clusterMaxZoom. Don't: Do not draw tens of thousands of overlapping point symbols. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/troubleshooting/working-with-large-geojson-data/)
- **S17 Cluster popups summarize** (medium): Do: Show the count, the dominant category and browse or zoom-to actions in a cluster popup. Don't: Do not open a popup listing every member of a large cluster. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/javascript/latest/api-reference/esri-smartMapping-popup-clusters.html)
- **S18 No-data gets its own legend class** (high, synth): Do: Give no-data or not-observed its own legend class (neutral grey, hatch or outline), distinct from "normal" and from zero. Don't: Do not paint missing, cloud-masked or not-observed features in the "normal" class.

## Layers

- **Y01 Only purposeful layers** (high): Do: Include only purposeful layers and turn non-essential layers off by default. Don't: Do not switch every available layer on at load. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)
- **Y02 Min/max visible scale on every layer** (high): Do: Set minimum and maximum visible scale (minzoom/maxzoom) on every layer. Don't: Do not draw detailed layers at world zoom or coarse layers at street zoom. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)
- **Y03 Grey out layers outside visible scale** (medium, synth): Do: Grey out layers whose zoom range excludes the current view and offer 'zoom to visible'. Don't: Do not leave a checked layer that silently draws nothing. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)
- **Y04 Human-readable titles and field aliases** (high): Do: Use human-readable layer titles and field aliases; hide OBJECTID and coded values. Don't: Do not expose raw field names like OBJECTID or code 3. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)
- **Y05 Layer order** (high): Do: Order bottom to top: basemap, thematic, reference lines and labels. Put raster under vector. Don't: Do not draw a raster over vector or labels, or boundaries under a fill. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-pro/mapping/primary-design-principles-for-cartography)
- **Y06 Per-layer opacity slider** (medium): Do: Offer a per-layer opacity slider, especially for rasters. Don't: Do not force users to choose between fully opaque and off. [source](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Applications/Browser.html)
- **Y07 Grouping layers** (medium, synth): Do: Use collapsible theme groups. Mutually exclusive rasters use radio buttons. Avoid a flat list of more than 15 checkboxes. Don't: Do not allow two opaque rasters on at once. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)

## Interaction

- **I01 Roth interaction operators** (medium): Do: Check the design against Roth's operators: pan, zoom, filter, search, retrieve, overlay, resymbolize, reexpress, sequence, arrange, calculate, reproject. Don't: Do not ship only pan and zoom when users need to filter, search and retrieve. [source](https://geography.wisc.edu/cartography/projects/publications/Roth_2013_TVCG.pdf)
- **I02 Hover highlights, click opens details** (high): Do: On hover change feature-state and the cursor; open the full popup only on click. Don't: Do not open a big popup on hover. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/example/hover-styles/)
- **I03 Clear the previous highlight** (medium): Do: Clear the previous highlight on mouseleave or when a new feature is selected. Don't: Do not leave stale highlighted features behind. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/example/hover-styles/)
- **I04 Curated popup fields** (high): Do: Show curated fields with aliases and formatted values, title first. Don't: Never dump every attribute into the popup. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/mapping/mapping/intelligent-maps-checklist-web-maps)
- **I05 Linked brushing across map, chart and table** (high, synth): Do: Keep one shared selection state and one highlight color across map, chart and table. Don't: Do not let each widget keep its own selection. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **I06 flyTo and reduced motion** (medium): Do: Use flyTo defaults speed 1.2 and curve 1.42; set maxDuration; honor prefers-reduced-motion (the animation becomes a jump unless essential:true). Don't: Do not fly across the globe for a nearby selection or ignore reduced-motion. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/api/properties/)
- **I07 Locator results list** (medium): Do: List name, distance, hours and Directions per result, and keep the list in sync with the markers. Don't: Do not show markers without a list or a list that ignores the map extent. [source](https://developers.google.com/maps/solutions/store-locator/best-practices)
- **I08 URL holds the view state** (medium, synth): Do: Store center, zoom, layers, time and selection in the URL. Don't: Do not make views unshareable or lose state on reload. [source](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/tutorials/embedded-worldview-examples)
- **I09 Show active filters** (high, synth): Do: Show filter chips and an 'N of M features shown' count with a clear-all action. Don't: Do not filter silently. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/javascript/latest/sample-code/featurereduction-cluster-filter/)

## Temporal

- **T01 Compare modes** (high): Do: Offer swipe as the default compare mode, with opacity fade and spy lens as alternatives. Don't: Do not make users toggle layers on and off to compare dates. [source](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/news/blog/introducing-worldviews-comparison-feature)
- **T02 Label the A/B dates on both halves** (high): Do: Label the date (and source) on both halves of a compare view. Don't: Do not leave the two sides unlabeled. [source](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/news/blog/introducing-worldviews-comparison-feature)
- **T03 Swipe controls** (medium): Do: Add swap and orientation controls; note that a legend may not apply to both sides. Don't: Do not assume one legend fits both sides. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/experience-builder/latest/configure-widgets/swipe-widget.htm)
- **T04 Time slider** (medium): Do: Use explicit stops, instant or window mode, a configurable rate and loop; pause playback while the view is loading. Don't: Do not advance frames while tiles are still loading. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/javascript/latest/api-reference/esri-widgets-TimeSlider.html)
- **T05 Timelapse export** (low): Do: Choose range, fps and frequency; export GIF or MP4; state which visualization is animated. Don't: Do not export an animation without date range and visualization labels. [source](https://cop4schools.readthedocs.io/en/latest/part4/4_1.html)
- **T06 Cloud cover filter** (high): Do: Provide a cloud cover filter with a default maximum of 30% (as in Copernicus Browser for Sentinel-2 L2A) and make it adjustable. Don't: Do not show all scenes regardless of cloud cover. [source](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Applications/Browser.html)
- **T07 Disclose that cloud % is per scene** (high): Do: State that cloud percent is per scene, not per AOI, or compute it for the AOI. Don't: Do not present scene cloud percent as AOI cloud percent. [source](https://www.sentinel-hub.com/explore/eobrowser/user-guide/)
- **T08 Date picker marks acquisition dates** (medium, synth): Do: Mark acquisition dates in the date picker and grey out dates with no data. Don't: Do not let users pick dates that return nothing. [source](https://www.sentinel-hub.com/explore/eobrowser/user-guide/)
- **T09 Time series flags cloudy observations** (high): Do: Flag or remove cloudy observations in time series charts. Don't: Do not plot cloud-contaminated values as real signal. [source](https://medium.com/sentinel-hub/multi-year-time-series-of-multi-spectral-data-viewed-and-analyzed-in-sentinel-hub-5628ec4fad9c)

## EO

- **E01 Name composites by purpose** (high): Do: Name composites by purpose: 'True color', 'False color (vegetation)', 'NDVI'. Don't: Never show bare band strings such as B08,B04,B03. [source](https://www.sentinel-hub.com/explore/eobrowser/user-guide/)
- **E02 S2 true color is B04/B03/B02** (medium): Do: Label Sentinel-2 B04/B03/B02 as true color and every other combination as false color. Don't: Do not call a false-color composite natural or true color. [source](https://gis4schools.readthedocs.io/en/latest/part3/rs_satelliteimages.html)
- **E03 Fixed index domains** (high, synth): Do: Use a fixed domain (-1 to 1 for NDVI and NDWI) with a ticked legend. Don't: Never auto-stretch an index per scene. [source](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/blogs/elegantfigures/2013/08/12/subtleties-of-color-part-3-of-6/)
- **E04 SAR in dB** (medium): Do: Display SAR in dB and use a VV, VH, VV-VH composite; explain that water is dark and urban is bright. Don't: Do not show raw linear SAR values without explanation. [source](https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/help/analysis/image-analyst/interpret-sar-data-for-flood-mapping.htm)
- **E05 Scene cards** (high): Do: Show a scene card with thumbnail, date, AOI coverage %, resolution, sensor and cloud %. Don't: Do not list scenes by ID only. [source](https://support.planet.com/hc/en-us/articles/212642197-Search-Criteria-and-Filters-in-Planet-Explorer)
- **E06 AOI coverage filter** (medium): Do: Provide an AOI coverage filter and show the footprint on hover. Don't: Do not return scenes that barely overlap the AOI without saying so. [source](https://developers.planet.com/docs/integrations/arcgis/v1/search)
- **E07 Mute imagery under detections** (medium): Do: Dim or desaturate imagery under detections or highlighted areas. Don't: Do not draw bright overlays on bright imagery. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-online/mapping/highlighting-areas-effects)
- **E08 Show AI detection confidence** (high, synth): Do: Show confidence in the popup and the list, add a threshold slider and encode it visually (opacity or dash). Don't: Do not show model detections as certain. [source](https://www.tableau.com/research/publications/value-suppressing-uncertainty-palettes)
- **E09 State the AIS match gate and time offset** (high, synth): Do: Interpolate AIS to the SAR acquisition time, and state the match radius and time tolerance (and the offset per pair) in the UI. Don't: Do not label a detection "no AIS match" without showing the gate that was used.

## Widgets

- **D01 A few KPIs with reference and units** (high): Do: Show a few KPIs, each with a reference value (previous period or target) and units. Don't: Do not show bare numbers without context. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **D02 Show data freshness** (high): Do: Show a 'last updated' timestamp and align the refresh interval with the layer refresh. Don't: Do not leave users guessing how current the data is. [source](https://support.esri.com/en-us/bug/the-indicator-widget-in-arcgis-dashboards-does-not-refl-bug-000147531)
- **D03 Pre-aggregate at the source** (high): Do: Pre-aggregate at the source and load only the needed fields. Don't: Do not pull all features to the client to compute totals. [source](https://www.esri.com/content/dam/esrisites/en-us/media/powerpoints/design-impactful-arcgis-dashboards-with-best-practices.pdf)
- **D04 Prefer attribute filters over extent filters** (medium): Do: Prefer attribute filters over map-extent filters on high-traffic dashboards. Don't: Do not rerun every widget query on every pan. [source](https://www.esri.com/content/dam/esrisites/en-us/events/conferences/2021/developer-summit/12527-arcgis-dashboards-tips-and-tricks.pdf)
- **D05 Limit relative-date filters at scale** (low): Do: Limit relative-date filters (last 7 days) on large, high-traffic dashboards. Don't: Do not make every widget use a unique relative date. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/build-highly-scalable-dashboards.htm)
- **D06 One categorical palette for map and charts** (high): Do: Use one categorical palette shared by the map and charts. Don't: Do not color the same category differently in two widgets. [source](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/color-palettes/)
- **D07 Label chart series directly** (low): Do: Label chart series directly instead of through a separate legend. Don't: Do not make users match colors between a distant legend and lines. [source](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/chart-anatomy/)
- **D08 Encode uncertainty** (high): Do: Encode uncertainty with a VSUP or a separate confidence channel. Don't: Do not show estimates with high error at the same visual certainty as reliable ones. [source](https://www.tableau.com/research/publications/value-suppressing-uncertainty-palettes)
- **D09 Alert list linked to map** (high, synth): Do: Sort alerts by severity and time; clicking an alert zooms and highlights it; the list scrolls to the item selected on the map. Don't: Do not make the list and the map independent. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **D10 Source and date per layer, readable without hover** (medium, synth): Do: Show each layer's source and acquisition date (or "date not published") in the legend or layer list, readable without hover. Don't: Do not put source and date in a truncated line or only in a hover tooltip.

## Layout

- **L01 Full vs partial map by task** (high): Do: Use a full-map layout for exploration and a map plus side panel for task-funneled workflows. Don't: Do not default to one layout without thinking about the user task. [source](https://resources.esri.ca/education-and-research/choosing-the-right-arcgis-app-builder-a-ux-ui-design-perspective-part-1)
- **L02 Primary panel on the reading-start side** (medium): Do: Put the primary panel on the reading-start side: left for LTR languages, mirrored for RTL. Don't: Do not put the main panel on the far side from where users start reading. [source](https://resources.esri.ca/education-and-research/choosing-the-right-arcgis-app-builder-a-ux-ui-design-perspective-part-1)
- **L03 Shell layout with action bar** (high): Do: Use a header, an action bar (icon rail) that opens collapsible panels, and the map in the content area. Don't: Do not stack always-open widgets over the map. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/calcite-design-system/tutorials/create-a-mapping-app)
- **L04 Action bar overflow** (medium): Do: Collapse the action bar into a 'more' menu when the screen is short. Don't: Do not clip tools silently when the viewport height shrinks. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/calcite-design-system/sample-code/app-adaptive-grid/)
- **L05 Size elements by importance** (high): Do: Make the most critical element (the map or the headline KPI) the largest on the page. Don't: Do not use a uniform tile grid where every element has the same weight. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **L06 Group related elements** (high): Do: Keep the legend next to the map and the details next to the list they describe. Don't: Do not scatter related elements across the page. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **L07 Audience and display target first** (high): Do: Define the audience and the display target first, then test on the real display (a wall screen vs a laptop). Don't: Do not design on a laptop only and assume it works on a wall display or projector. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **L08 Only what users need** (high): Do: Include only what users need to answer their questions and leave out nice-to-have charts. Don't: Do not add widgets just because the data allows it. [source](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/dashboards/latest/reference/author-effective-dashboards.htm)
- **L09 Mobile layout: map on top, bottom sheet** (high): Do: Below 768px put the map on top (about 70vh) and a bottom sheet (30vh or less) underneath, with touch-sized controls. Don't: Do not squeeze a side panel next to the map on a phone. [source](https://developers.arcgis.com/documentation/app-builders/low-code/arcgis-experience-builder/build-mobile-solutions/)
- **L10 Short title and an about panel** (medium): Do: Use a short title and an about panel that briefly explains the app purpose and data. Don't: Do not leave users guessing what the map shows or where the data came from. [source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/configurable-apps/sharing-collaboration/configure-apps-with-mobile-in-mind)
- **L11 Z-order of map and UI layers** (high, synth): Do: Stack bottom to top: basemap, thematic, reference labels/boundaries, selection highlight, controls, panels, popups, modals/toasts. Keep attribution uncovered. Don't: Do not let panels, legends or toasts cover the attribution or the selection highlight. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/dive-deeper/attribution/)
- **L12 Map-first vs data-first** (medium, synth): Do: Use map-first when the question is 'where?'. Use list-first for lookup tasks, sorted by distance or time. Don't: Do not force a map when users really need to find one item in a list. [source](https://developers.google.com/maps/solutions/store-locator/best-practices)

## Performance

- **P01 Large data goes to vector tiles** (high): Do: Move large datasets to vector tiles (Mapbox Tiling Service, PMTiles or MVT). Docs suggest extra techniques above about 500,000 data points. Don't: Do not load huge GeoJSON files into the browser. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/troubleshooting/working-with-large-geojson-data/)
- **P02 Slim GeoJSON** (high): Do: Keep at most 6 coordinate decimals, only the needed properties, load by URL and set source maxzoom below 18 (default 18; the docs suggest 12 for most point sources). Don't: Do not inline large GeoJSON objects or carry unused properties. [source](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/troubleshooting/working-with-large-geojson-data/)
- **P03 Clustering with supercluster** (high): Do: Use supercluster for client-side clustering and render points as layers. Don't: Do not create per-point DOM markers beyond a few hundred points. [source](https://blog.mapbox.com/clustering-millions-of-points-on-a-map-with-supercluster-272046ec5c97)
- **P04 Feedback by response time** (medium): Do: Under 0.1 s show nothing; around 1 s still no indicator (wait about 1 s before showing one); 2 to 10 s a spinner or skeleton; over 10 s a percent-done bar. Don't: Do not flash a spinner for fast responses. [source](https://www.uxtigers.com/post/progress-indicators)
- **P05 Skeletons for panels, thin bar for the map** (low, synth): Do: Use skeletons for panels with known layout (about 3 s or less) and a thin bar or fade-in for the map. Don't: Do not blank the map with a full-screen spinner. [source](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/loading-pattern)
- **P06 Per-layer error state with retry** (high, synth): Do: Show an error state with retry on the failing layer; other layers keep working. Don't: Do not fail the entire map for one layer.
- **P07 Scale-dependent rendering and simplification** (medium, synth): Do: Simplify geometry and render features dependent on scale. Don't: Do not send full-resolution geometry to a small-scale view.
